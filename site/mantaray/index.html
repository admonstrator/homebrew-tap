What it does

Switches know exactly how your network hangs together. mantaray gets it out of them — without configuring, changing or stressing anything.

🫧

Passive by design

Reads STP/RSTP/MSTP and PVST+ BPDUs, LLDP and CDP straight off the wire. Strictly read-only: no SETs, no BPDUs of its own, no seat at the spanning-tree table.

🗺️

A live map

Box-drawing topology with utilisation-weighted links, paint modes (m) for health, utilisation, VLAN and vendor, a radial layout (r), drag-to-pin nodes — full mouse support throughout.

🔌

SNMP enrichment

v1, v2c and v3: port tables, speeds, VLANs, MAC/FDB tables and LLDP neighbours complete the picture that frames alone can't show — auto-discovered from the traffic it hears.

🩺

The doctor

Duplex, MTU and native-VLAN mismatches, half-duplex uplinks, error storms, legacy STP, roots in the wrong place — every finding scored into a 0–100 health figure with a fix hint.

📈

Traffic

An aggregate braille throughput chart, top talkers with per-port trends, and a per-switch front panel where hot or error-spewing ports light up.

🧪

What-if

Fail a switch, cut a link or change a bridge priority (W) and watch how the spanning tree would re-converge — before you touch the real thing.

📡

Rogue radar

Unexpected DHCP servers and IPv6 router advertisements, MAC moves between ports and duplicate IPs surface as events the moment they appear on the wire.

📄

Reports & exports

A self-contained HTML report, SVG/DOT/Mermaid/CSV exports, sessions that save and load as JSON, and pcap import for replaying captures — no root required.

🤖

Headless mode

--headless captures for a while, scores the network and writes the report — perfect for a jump host or a scheduled health check.

A closer look

Real screens from a live capture — one colour language everywhere: green is fine, amber is worth a look, red is tonight's problem.

Also a plain CLI

No TTY, no problem — headless runs capture, diagnose and report on their own.

$ mantaray --headless --duration 300 --report office.html
mantaray: headless capture for 300s …
mantaray: 7 switch(es), 7 link(s), 182 endpoint(s) — health 86/100, 0 critical finding(s)
mantaray: report → office.html

$ mantaray --replay yesterday.pcap  # replay a capture — no privileges needed

Install

Via this tap for macOS on Apple Silicon and Linux on x86_64 and arm64. Prebuilt binaries — nothing compiles on your machine.

$ brew tap admonstrator/tap
$ brew trust admonstrator/tap
$ brew install mantaray
🪟

Windows

Grab the x86_64-pc-windows-msvc zip from the releases page. Live capture needs Npcap installed separately — mantaray doesn't bundle it.

🔑

Capture privileges

Listening on an interface needs raw-socket rights: sudo (or ChmodBPF) on macOS, setcap cap_net_raw,cap_net_admin=eip on Linux. Replays, sessions and reports run fully unprivileged.

🔭

See more, passively

On a SPAN/mirror port or a TAP, mantaray sees per-VLAN BPDUs and far more traffic — the honest way to widen the view without ever transmitting.
