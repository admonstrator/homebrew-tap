A TUI network topology mapper: mantaray listens to STP, LLDP and CDP on the wire, fills in the blanks over SNMP, and draws your switches, links and their warts — live, in your terminal.

Install Download binaries

What it does

Switches know exactly how your network hangs together. mantaray gets it out of them — without configuring, changing or stressing anything.

🫧

Passive by design

Reads STP/RSTP/MSTP and PVST+ BPDUs, LLDP, CDP, VTP, DTP, UDLD and LACP straight off the wire. Strictly read-only: no SETs, no BPDUs of its own, no seat at the spanning-tree table.

🗺️

A live map

Utilisation-weighted links with live traffic animation, STP-blocked reserve paths drawn dashed with both ends named, paint modes (m) for health, utilisation, VLAN and vendor, a radial layout (r), drag-to-pin — full mouse support throughout.

🧭

Discover & enrich

One keypress sweeps a subnet with ARP/ICMP, then SNMP-probes every responder (v1, v2c, v3) — port tables, speeds, VLANs, FDB and LLDP neighbours fill the map in on their own.

🔎

Fullscreen port view

Enter on a switch shows its ports laid out like the face of the device, above a table whose columns cycle (v) through basic, VLAN, STP, traffic and neighbour facts. Enter on a port jumps across the link into the neighbour's view.

🩺

The doctor

Duplex, MTU and native-VLAN mismatches, error storms, broadcast storms, legacy STP islands, roots in the wrong place, portfast reminders — every finding scored into a 0–100 health figure with a fix hint.

📈

Traffic

An aggregate braille throughput chart, top talkers with per-port trends, and a front panel that doubles as a heatmap — hot or error-spewing ports light up.

🌓

Two temperaments

M flips discovery ↔ monitoring: discovery runs the active machinery and judges “offline” leniently; monitoring stops every sweep and instead pings known switches gently, alarming after N misses.

🧪

What-if

Fail a switch, cut a link or change a bridge priority (W) and watch how the spanning tree would re-converge — entirely offline, before you touch the real thing.

📡

Rogue radar

Unexpected DHCP servers and IPv6 router advertisements, MAC flapping and duplicate IPs raise alarms; passive mDNS/SSDP/DHCP fingerprinting names endpoints and files them as printer, camera, phone …

🕰️

Timeline & drift

Scrub the session's history on a time axis (H), snapshot a baseline (b) and ask later what changed (B): new or lost switches and links, root moves, VLAN changes.

🚨

Alert channels

Every alarm can go out as RFC 5424 syslog over UDP and/or into a per-alarm shell hook that gets the event as JSON on stdin — curl -d @- turns that into any webhook.

📄

Reports & exports

A self-contained HTML report, SVG/DOT/Mermaid/CSV exports, versioned sessions that save and load, pcap replay — and a --headless mode that captures, scores and reports without a TTY.

A closer look

Real screens from a live capture — one colour language everywhere: green is fine, amber is worth a look, red is tonight's problem.

Also a plain CLI

No TTY, no problem — headless runs capture, diagnose and report on their own.

$ mantaray --headless --duration 300 --report office.html
mantaray: headless capture for 300s …
mantaray: 7 switch(es), 7 link(s), 182 endpoint(s) — health 86/100, 0 critical finding(s)
mantaray: report → office.html

$ mantaray --replay yesterday.pcap  # replay a capture — no privileges needed

Install

Via this tap for macOS on Apple Silicon and Linux on x86_64 and arm64. Prebuilt binaries — nothing compiles on your machine.

$ brew tap admonstrator/tap
$ brew trust admonstrator/tap
$ brew install mantaray
🪟

Windows

Grab the x86_64-pc-windows-msvc zip from the releases page. Live capture needs Npcap installed separately — mantaray doesn't bundle it.

🔑

Capture privileges

Listening on an interface needs raw-socket rights: sudo (or ChmodBPF) on macOS, setcap cap_net_raw,cap_net_admin=eip on Linux. Replays, sessions and reports run fully unprivileged.

🔭

See more, passively

On a SPAN/mirror port or a TAP, mantaray sees per-VLAN BPDUs and far more traffic — the honest way to widen the view without ever transmitting.
